Google Calendar

Read-only. Your events become fixed blobs that Blob never moves, and it re-checks every 5 minutes. Add as many Google accounts as you like, then switch individual calendars on or off. Uses your own Google Cloud “Desktop app” OAuth client (setup steps are in the README).

Calendar links

For any calendar that gives you an iCal link (Google's “secret address in iCal format”, Outlook, iCloud, a shared team calendar). Links sync every 5 minutes in the Mac app.

Spotify

Shows what's playing at the top of BLOB, and plays the music you pick for each kind of blob when its timer starts. Seeing what's playing works on any account; playing, pausing and skipping need Spotify Premium. Uses your own Spotify app's client ID (README).

Sounds

Sound bites and catchphrases

Give a moment your own clip and lines. Upload audio or video, trim it, and Blob plays it instead of the built-in sound.
